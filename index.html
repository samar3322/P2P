  1. Set Your Identity: Click the person icon (👤) in the top-right corner to choose your nickname and a personal color for your avatar. This is how friends will see you.
  2. Connect With a Friend: This app is peer-to-peer, meaning one person initiates and the other scans.
    • To Start a Chat (and be scanned): Click the "Show My Code" card. A unique QR code will appear on your screen. Have your friend scan this code with their device.
    • To Join a Chat (by scanning): Click the "Scan QR Code" card and point your camera at the QR code displayed on your friend's screen.
  3. Chat Securely: Once the connection is made, you'll be taken to the chat screen. All messages are sent directly between you and your friend, with no server in the middle.
  4. Toggle Your Theme: Click the mountain icon in the header at any time to switch between a light and dark theme for your comfort.
